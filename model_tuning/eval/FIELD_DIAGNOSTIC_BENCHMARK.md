# Grow Doc Field Diagnostic Benchmark v0

Status: design contract only; no protected cases are frozen by this change.

## Purpose

Evaluate real-world Grow Doc diagnostic behavior without leaking benchmark material into SFT, grounded-QA, retrieval tuning, QLoRA/LoRA, adapter selection, or model-soup construction.

This benchmark is separate from Heldout-v2/v3. It targets multimodal field reliability: observation discipline, differential reasoning, uncertainty/abstention, visual grounding, evidence applicability, citation support, and robustness to domain shift.

## Required case families

A future frozen release must contain independently reviewed cases spanning:

- clean representative images;
- field/domain-shift images (lighting, blur, occlusion, canopy context, mixed backgrounds);
- early and late symptom progression;
- look-alike differentials;
- multiple simultaneous stressors;
- healthy and mechanically damaged controls;
- out-of-distribution/unsupported conditions;
- insufficient-information cases where calibrated abstention is required;
- adversarial retrieval with plausible irrelevant or conflicting evidence;
- temporal/multi-image cases;
- localization-annotated cases when defensible masks or regions are available.

No case family may be populated from training examples or generated by paraphrasing protected prompts.

## Three-pass diagnostic contract

1. **Observation:** record visible evidence only. Do not diagnose.
2. **Differential:** rank plausible causes from the observation record plus allowed context/retrieval.
3. **Verification/adjudication:** test the differential against source-bound evidence, applicability constraints, contradictions, and uncertainty.

A usable image remains analyzable without optional grow metadata. Missing optional metadata may lower confidence or change recommended verification steps, but must not block visual observation.

## Structured observations

The case schema supports organ/tissue, plant location, spatial distribution, chlorosis/necrosis patterns, lesion morphology, deformation, wilting, stem/flower signs, visible pests, severity, image quality, OOD flags, localization annotations, and observational uncertainty.

Observations must distinguish:
- directly visible facts;
- inferred but visually supported descriptors;
- unavailable/unknown information.

## Evidence and applicability

Each evidence item must retain source identity/citation bytes and may record species, cultivar, growth stage, environment, medium, treatment, study type, sample size, geography, publication year, evidence tier, directness, and claim scope.

Retrieved evidence is not automatically applicable. Scoring must penalize unsupported transfer across species, cultivar, growth stage, cultivation system, treatment, or study scope.

Predicted molecular structures (for example AlphaFold records) may support structural retrieval but cannot by themselves establish protein function, phenotype, disease mechanism, nutrient response, or cultivation recommendations.

## Uncertainty and abstention

Protected cases must include examples where the best answer is not a single confident diagnosis.

Required outputs include:
- ranked differential;
- calibrated confidence or confidence band;
- explicit ambiguity/insufficient-evidence state;
- what additional observation/test would discriminate leading alternatives;
- no invented certainty from missing pH/EC/environment metadata.

## Retrieval/citation contract

Scientific factual claims should be retrieval-grounded rather than memorized into weights when practical. Evaluation should atomize answer claims and test:
- whether a supporting source was retrieved;
- whether the cited passage supports the exact claim;
- whether evidence is applicable to the case;
- whether contradictory evidence was acknowledged;
- whether unsupported claims were introduced.

## Core metrics

Report per slice, not only aggregate:
- top-1 and top-k differential accuracy/recall;
- critical differential miss rate;
- overconfident-wrong rate;
- selective accuracy vs coverage / abstention quality;
- calibration (Brier/ECE where confidence is numeric);
- observation precision/recall against reviewed findings;
- localization overlap/pointing accuracy where annotations exist;
- claim support precision/recall;
- citation correctness and source applicability;
- hallucination/unsupported-claim rate;
- retrieval context precision/recall/utilization;
- robustness under domain shift and evidence noise.

Promotion must never hide a regression in diagnostic safety, hallucination resistance, citation correctness, or abstention behavior behind an aggregate gain.

## Data isolation

All future case records carry `evaluation_only_never_training: true`.

Frozen benchmark IDs, prompts, media, expected observations, adjudication notes, answer keys, source-bound labels, and semantically equivalent paraphrases are excluded from:
- SFT;
- grounded-QA training;
- retrieval reranker supervision;
- QLoRA/LoRA training;
- adapter combination/model-soup construction;
- prompt tuning.

Retrieval corpora may contain independently published scientific sources referenced by benchmark cases, but not benchmark labels/adjudication material.

## Versioning

The eventual freeze process must produce:
- immutable JSONL case artifact;
- media manifest with content hashes and license/provenance;
- source-review manifest;
- benchmark SHA-256;
- construction-input hashes;
- contamination report;
- slice-coverage report.

This v0 document and schema define the contract only. They do not claim that cases, images, masks, training runs, or model evaluations exist.
