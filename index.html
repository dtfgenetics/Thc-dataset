THC Grow Doc

Use structured symptoms, grow context, image or video observations, reference evidence, and transparent confidence bands to compare possible cannabis plant-health causes. Visual evidence alone does not confirm disease or nutrient disorders.

How Grow Doc works

  1. Observe: start with a whole-plant or affected-area image and describe what is visibly changing.
  2. Add context: include plant stage, growing system, environment, irrigation, root-zone readings, and recent changes when available.
  3. Compare causes: review plausible differentials together with evidence for, evidence against, and important missing observations.
  4. Verify: use the safest next observation or measurement before treating one visual symptom as proof of a specific cause.

Evidence boundary

Grow Doc is an educational decision-support tool. Photos can document visible features, but laboratory testing or other confirmation may be required for pathogens, viroids, and other conditions that cannot be verified from appearance alone.
